CHAPTER 05 — ABOUT
Purpose

The About section is not a biography.

It is the moment where curiosity becomes trust.

The Hero successfully captures attention.

The About section must justify that attention.

Visitors should leave this section understanding not only who the developer is, but also how they think.

The objective is to communicate mindset before experience.

Story Role

Within the portfolio narrative, this section answers one question.

Why should I trust this person?

The answer should not rely on years of experience or impressive claims.

Instead, trust should emerge from clarity, honesty, craftsmanship, and attention to detail.

The visitor should feel they are meeting the creator behind the product.

Emotional Goal

The visitor should experience the following progression.

Curiosity

↓

Understanding

↓

Connection

↓

Trust

↓

Continue Scrolling

This section should feel calm.

The pace intentionally slows after the cinematic Hero.

The portfolio gives the visitor a moment to breathe before introducing technical capabilities.

Transition From Hero

The transition between Hero and About is one continuous scene.

The Hero should not disappear.

It should evolve.

The frame-based character completes its cinematic movement and naturally settles into the About section.

The visitor should never perceive a page break.

Instead, they should feel that the camera has simply arrived at the next scene.

This continuity is one of the portfolio's defining characteristics.

Character Placement

The character remains visible throughout the About section.

However, the character no longer dominates the composition.

Its role changes.

In the Hero, the character introduces itself.

In About, the character becomes a visual companion while the visitor reads.

The character remains calm.

No additional animations beyond subtle breathing and environmental movement.

Layout Philosophy

The About section follows a balanced two-column composition.

Left

↓

Personal Story
Section Heading
Description
Primary Information

|

Right

↓

Character
At a Glance Card
Statistics
Quick Information

The layout should feel stable.

Nothing should overlap unnecessarily.

The visitor's eye should move naturally from left to right.

Content Philosophy

The About section is not a résumé.

Avoid chronological storytelling.

Instead, communicate identity.

The writing should answer questions like:

What motivates this developer?
How do they approach problems?
What makes their work different?
What kind of products do they enjoy building?
Why do they care about user experience?

The writing should sound human.

Never exaggerated.

Never corporate.

Writing Tone

The tone should be:

Confident.

Honest.

Professional.

Curious.

Thoughtful.

Avoid:

Buzzwords.

Corporate jargon.

Self-praise.

Marketing language.

Generic AI-generated phrases.

The visitor should feel that the text could only belong to this individual.

Heading

The section heading should be short.

Simple.

Confident.

Examples of acceptable structure:

About

About Me

Who I Am

Avoid long marketing-style headings.

Description

The primary description should explain the developer's philosophy rather than listing achievements.

Preferred themes include:

Building products.

Solving real problems.

Attention to detail.

Creating memorable experiences.

Engineering with purpose.

Design thinking.

Artificial Intelligence.

Continuous learning.

At A Glance Card

The At A Glance card summarizes key information.

It should never replace the written story.

Instead, it supports it.

The card exists because recruiters often scan before reading.

This component allows both behaviors.

Card Philosophy

The card should resemble a premium dashboard panel.

Compact.

Organized.

Easy to scan.

Every item receives equal spacing.

No visual clutter.

Internal Layout

The At A Glance card occupies a fixed visual height.

Approximately 255px.

Inside this card are four vertically stacked information cards.

Each mini-card contains:

Icon

↓

Label

↓

Value

All four mini-cards share identical height and spacing.

No card should appear visually heavier than another.

Information Categories

Recommended information includes:

Location

Education

Current Focus

Availability

These may evolve over time without changing the layout.

Mini Card Design

Every mini-card follows the global glass material.

Padding remains consistent.

Icons align perfectly.

Text alignment never changes.

Hover interaction remains subtle.

Small brightness increase.

Tiny shadow enhancement.

No scaling.

Statistics

If numerical statistics are displayed, they should represent meaningful information.

Examples:

Projects Built

Technologies

Years Learning

Certifications

Avoid vanity metrics.

Quality is more important than quantity.

Visual Hierarchy

Information order should always be:

Heading

↓

Description

↓

At A Glance

↓

Statistics

↓

Secondary Details

The user should understand the story before reading facts.

Motion

The About section intentionally uses restrained animation.

Allowed:

Soft fade-ins.

Slide transitions.

Glass reflections.

Tiny floating movement.

Character breathing.

Background gradient drift.

Avoid:

Large scene transitions.

Explosions.

Particle systems.

Fast animations.

The Hero already provided the cinematic impact.

The About section builds trust through stability.

Background

The global background continues seamlessly.

No new colors.

No different lighting.

No visual reset.

The visitor should feel they remain inside the same world.

Glass Material

All cards follow the Design System.

Never introduce:

Different blur values.

Different corner radius.

Different border opacity.

Different shadows.

Consistency creates premium quality.

Responsive Behaviour

Desktop:

Two-column layout.

Tablet:

Columns become slightly narrower while preserving balance.

Mobile:

Character moves above the content.

Cards become full width.

Reading experience always takes priority.

Accessibility

All text must maintain strong contrast.

Cards must remain readable even when blur is unavailable.

Keyboard navigation must include every interactive element.

Reduced motion removes only decorative animation.

Hierarchy remains identical.

Success Criteria

The About section succeeds when visitors think:

"This developer clearly cares about quality."

before they have even reached the Skills section.

Trust should be established through experience rather than claims.

If visitors naturally continue scrolling because they want to see proof of these ideas, the About section has fulfilled its purpose.
